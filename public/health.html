Food safety

Daily operational records designed for routine food safety management and inspection readiness.

ACT control points: potentially hazardous food should be kept at 5°C or colder or 60°C or hotter. ACT guidance also requires relevant food safety records to be kept for at least 3 months where prescribed activities apply.

Cold storage

Fridges, coolroom, cold display. Pass ≤ 5°C.

Hot holding

Bain marie / hot display. Pass ≥ 60°C.

Frozen storage

House target ≤ −18°C; also check food remains frozen hard.

Cooking / reheating / manual

Record measured internal temperature and action if unsafe.

Food receiving temperature

For potentially hazardous chilled or hot deliveries. Record supplier, item and measured temperature.

Receiving rule

Cold potentially hazardous food is accepted at 5°C or below, hot food at 60°C or above. Frozen food should be frozen hard. Any rejection or corrective action is recorded.

Sanitiser check

Record chemical, concentration/reading and whether it meets the product instructions.

Dishwasher / heat sanitising

Record machine verification or service check. For manual hot-water sanitising, ACT guidance uses 77°C for at least 30 seconds.

Probe thermometer check

Food thermometer should be food-grade, probe type and accurate to ±1°C. Record calibration / accuracy check.

Cleaning procedure

Clean first, then sanitise. Food-contact surfaces and utensils must be clean and sanitary; record repairs and maintenance that affect hygiene.
