FOOD SAFETYINSPECTION PACK

Critical control records

Cooling, time control, cleaning, pest and corrective actions. Records feed the same food-safety history used for inspection preparation.

Use this for cooked potentially hazardous food that is cooled for later use. Record start and follow-up temperatures/times and any action taken.

Cooling record

What to record

Use measured temperatures, not estimates. If the cooling process is outside your safe process, record the corrective action, such as rapid chilling, reheating for immediate service, or disposal.

Time control record

2-hour / 4-hour rule

Record when potentially hazardous food is deliberately held between 5°C and 60°C. The record should make it clear how long the food has been out of temperature control and what was done with it.

Cleaning verification

Routine schedule

Use this for jobs beyond normal wipe-downs: coolroom, exhaust surfaces, drains, walls, shelving, under equipment, dishwashing area, waste area and any recurring deep-clean task.

Pest escalation

Any evidence affecting food or food-contact areas should create a clear trail: observation → immediate containment → contractor/repair action → closure.
